What we offer

Services built for everyday health needs

From routine screening to specialised referrals and home visits, our team is here for the whole family — in the clinic and out in the community.

Core healthcare services

Comprehensive primary & specialised care

Tailored to the everyday needs of the community we serve.

Infectious diseases

Malaria testing and comprehensive treatment; tuberculosis (TB) screening, diagnostic referral, and monitoring.

HIV care & support

Voluntary counselling, confidential testing, ongoing psychosocial support, and seamless linkage to care.

Chronic disease management

Regular hypertension and diabetes screening, continuous patient monitoring, and chronic disease treatment plans.

General medicine

Diagnosis and clinical management of common acute illnesses for adults and children.

Health education & prevention

Community-wide health awareness, preventative care instruction, and health counselling.

Family planning

Judgement-free counselling and a range of family planning options for every stage of life.

From routine checkups to specialised referrals

Every visit starts with listening — then the right level of care, in the clinic or at home.

Community outreach program

Care that comes to you

Through regular medical camps, mobile screening units, and home visits, we identify and address health challenges at the grass-roots level.

Preventive healthcare & early diagnosis

Timely screening and early detection of diseases to prevent complications.

Specialised care referrals

Facilitating rapid referral paths for patients requiring specialised medical intervention.

Focus on vulnerable groups

Targeted assistance for low-income families, young children, the elderly, and individuals with physical limitations.

Home visits

Long-term home-based care services for patients unable to travel to static clinic facilities.
